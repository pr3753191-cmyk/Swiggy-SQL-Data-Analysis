# Swiggy SQL Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Swiggy's customer, restaurant, order, and delivery partner data using SQL.

The objective is to solve business-related questions and generate useful insights from the available data.

## 🎯 Business Questions

The project answers 13 business questions related to:

1. Customers living in Delhi
2. Average restaurant rating in Mumbai
3. Customers who have placed at least one order
4. Total orders placed by each customer
5. Total revenue generated by each restaurant
6. Top 5 restaurants with the highest average rating
7. Customers who have never placed an order
8. Orders placed by each customer in Mumbai
9. Orders placed in the last 30 days
10. Delivery partners who completed more than 1 delivery
11. Customers who placed orders on exactly three different days
12. Delivery partner who worked with the most different customers
13. Customers with the same city and restaurant but different order dates
